# Master Privacy Policy: TIP

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

TIP ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TIP client portal, artist dispatch platform, and management ecosystem (All rights reserved. TIP Client, TIP Artist, and TIP HQ are proprietary platforms and our companion mobile applications). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information across our platform to provide, secure, and streamline custom tattoo bookings, artist dispatches, and management operations:

* **Personal Identifiers:** Full name, first name, last name, email address, phone number, account IDs, client UUIDs, artist IDs, shop name, location, and professional license details.
* **Staff & Management Data:** For internal management and staff applicants, we collect department information, experience years, resume files/URLs, bios, application statuses, and administrative review decisions.
* **Sensitive Personal Data:** Government-issued ID documents and photo IDs (uploaded by artists and clients for mandatory compliance verification), verified legal gender (used exclusively for gender-preferred dispatch routing), date of birth inputs, and professional compliance certifications (CPR certificates, first-aid certificates, and bloodborne-pathogen training records).
* **Precise Location Data:** GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude), station coordinates, job location addresses, ZIP/postal codes, state metadata, and travel distance calculations used for geofenced job broadcasting.
* **Financial & Transaction Information:** Budgets, deposits, platform fees, artist payouts, balance due, tips, refund records, cashout notes/timestamps, and Stripe transaction/payment identifiers (PaymentIntents, checkout sessions, transfer IDs) processed securely via Stripe.
* **User Content & Booking Details:** Tattoo and piercing descriptions, style preferences, placement details, placement sides, reference links, reference images, portfolio photos, flash-drop merchandise designs, child-piercing flags, child age in months, parental consent statuses, appointment notes, ratings, written comments, and support messages.
* **Device & Technical Data:** IP addresses, browser user agents, session cookies, authentication tokens, request origins, error logs, audit logs (tracking actor actions, target entities, and serialized changes), webhook event IDs, and local device storage markers (localStorage and mobile AsyncStorage tokens).

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following operational purposes:

* **Account Management & Verification:** Registering accounts, reviewing professional credentials and government IDs, and managing administrative approvals or rejections.
* **Job Dispatch & Matching:** Connecting clients with nearby available artists, processing geofenced broadcasts, and executing gender-preferred dispatch routing.
* **Payment Processing:** Facilitating secure financial transactions, platform fee calculations, artist payouts, cashouts, and refunds through Stripe.
* **Safety & Compliance:** Enforcing platform safety policies, preventing fraud, maintaining immutable audit logs, and resolving support inquiries.

### 3. Third-Party Data Sharing & Infrastructure

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted third-party service providers necessary to operate our platform infrastructure:

* **Stripe:** For secure payment processing, fund transfers, payouts, and financial compliance.
* **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap:** Receiving client GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) for public reverse-geocoding to establish service regions and ZIP mappings.
* **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety and rights of our users and platform.

### 4. Data Security & Retention

* **Security Measures:** We utilize encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure server-side validations, database transaction controls, and restricted administrative access to protect your data. Government IDs, sensitive credentials, and mobile authentication tokens are protected via secure storage guidelines.
* **Retention & Deletion:** You may initiate account deletion directly within your in-app account settings. Upon request, personal identifiers are immediately scrubbed or anonymized. However, to comply with applicable tax, financial, and accounting obligations, certain transaction records, payout logs, and mandatory audit trails are securely retained for up to 7 years.

### 5. Your Choices & Rights

* **Location Permissions:** You can enable or disable location tracking at any time through your device or browser settings. Note that disabling location may limit your ability to post mobile jobs or receive local dispatches.
* **Account Information:** You can update your profile details and preferences directly within your client or artist account settings.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18, except for minor piercing requests accompanied by verified parental/guardian consent and age-verification tracking managed through our platform workflows.

### 7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through your management support portal or at **redartcincy@gmail.com**.
